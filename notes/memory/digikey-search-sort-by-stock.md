When searching Digi-Key, sort the results by in-stock quantity, descending, so the most common (best-stocked, usually cheapest and longest-lived) parts come first.

**Why:** the user's own practice (2026-11-14); it surfaces mainstream parts instead of odd ones or discontinued listings.

**How to apply:** combine with the user's standing purchasing rules for this project: in stock at Digi-Key, not Marketplace, orderable in small quantity, consolidate values/parts. Related: [[replacement-plan-obi-glasgow]].
