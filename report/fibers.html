The four EO fibers (confirmed by capture)

The EO board has one HFBR-1521 transmitter and three HFBR-2521 receivers (HP/Agilent Versatile Link, 5 MBd). Captures of all four with a Tektronix MSO24 during a cold boot and while operating every LEO-32 control settled their roles. All are TTL idle high at the transceivers.
